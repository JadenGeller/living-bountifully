Cadiz was one of the first cities I explored after moving to Spain in the Andalusia region. The city reminds me of San Francisco due to its geographical layout and the prominent bridge spanning its entrance—the tallest in Spain.

Old Town Cadiz offers the most historic and walkable experience. You can complete major attractions in a single day, though I recommend a more leisurely pace rather than rushing through sites.

## Things to Do in Cadiz

1. Visit the Roman Theatre
2. Check out the Cadiz Cathedral
3. Climb to the top of the Tavira Tower
4. Take a stroll through Genoves Park
5. Indulge your senses at The Central Market of Cadiz
6. Explore the history of the city at the Cadiz Museum
7. Walk along the water to the Santa Catalina Castle
8. Roam through the San Sebastian Castle
9. Take a guided bike tour through Old Town
10. Experience local cuisine on a guided tapas tour
