One of the most exciting parts about living in New England is having four defined seasons. Having moved from Northern California, I want to prioritize experiencing each season fully. August has passed quickly and I created a seasonal bucket list to ensure comprehensive seasonal engagement.

Having four distinct seasons easily makes New England a year-round travel destination. Each season offers unique activities and experiences, from slow winters to vibrant spring renewal, endless summer adventures, and festive fall atmosphere.

## Spring

### Blooming Gardens & Flower Festivals (March to July)

Notable locations include:

- **Boston Common and Public Garden** - daffodils late March-early April, cherry blossoms mid-April, tulips early May
- **Nantucket Daffodil Festival** - April
- **Cape Cod Hydrangea Festival** - July, Heritage Museums and Gardens

### Maple Sugaring (Late February to early April)

Recommended sugar houses:

- Shelburne Farms (Vermont)
- North Hadley Sugar Shack (Massachusetts)
- Bascom Maple Farms (New Hampshire)
- Lamothe's Sugar House (Connecticut)

## Summer

![Summer in New England](/images/new-england-summer.jpg)

### Explore Boston

I have been pleasantly surprised by how much there is to do in the city during the Summer. Activities include:

- Freedom Trail walking tour with food/drink stops at Faneuil Hall, Bell in Hand (America's oldest tavern), and the North End (Mike's Pastry for dessert)
- Free concerts and movies at Hatch Shell on the Esplanade
- Picnics in Public Garden or walks through the Common
- Broadway shows

### Outdoor Activities

- Hiking - Blue Hills near Boston, Worlds End in Hingham, Acadia National Park in Maine
- Paddleboarding
- Boating
- Biking - Cape Cod Rail Trail highlighted
- River tubing - Saco River, New Hampshire

### Beach Time

- Cape Cod National Seashore (I visited Marconi Beach for my birthday)
- Martha's Vineyard
- Nantucket
- Block Island (Rhode Island)
- Ogunquit (Maine)
- Sand Beach in Bar Harbor (Maine)
- Lake Winnipesaukee (New Hampshire)
- Echo Lake (New Hampshire)

## Fall

![Fall foliage in New England](/images/new-england-fall.jpg)

### Foliage Tour

Peak season is September and October. Recommendations:

- Kancamagus Highway (New Hampshire)
- Conway Scenic Railroad (New Hampshire)
- White Mountains (New Hampshire) or Acadia National Park (Maine) hiking

### Apple Picking & Cider Donuts

Recommended orchards:

- Cider Hill Farm (Massachusetts)
- Honey Pot Orchards (Massachusetts)
- Smolak Farms (Massachusetts)
- Shelburne Orchards (Vermont)
- Carter Hill Orchard (New Hampshire)

### Pumpkin Patch & Festival

Pumpkin patches:

- Treworgy Family Orchards (Maine)
- Fletcher Farm (Massachusetts)
- Lyman Orchards (Connecticut)

Pumpkin festivals:

- Jack-O-Lantern Spectacular in Providence, Rhode Island (September 26–November 1, 2025)
- New Hampshire Pumpkin Festival in Laconia (October 25, 2025)

### Spooky Season in Salem

Salem is the ultimate Halloween destination with Hocus Pocus filming locations. Check out the Salem Haunted Happenings website for more information.

## Winter

![Winter in New England](/images/new-england-winter.jpg)

### Skiing

Having not skied in years (Spain and San Diego lack ski infrastructure), I'm excited to try these resorts:

- Stowe Mountain Resort (Vermont)
- Sugarloaf (Maine)
- Bretton Woods (New Hampshire)

### Ice Skating

I prefer outdoor frozen ponds and lakes over rinks. Frog Pond in Boston Common is iconic.

### Festive Events

Holiday markets:

- Holiday Market at Snowport (Boston)
- Amherst German Christmas Market (New Hampshire)
- Plymouth Holiday Market

Christmas light displays:

- Gardens Aglow (Boothbay, Maine)
- Night Lights (Boylston, Massachusetts)
- Additional Boston-area displays

As the seasons have warmed, so have I to life in New England. This bucket list represents my initial exploration and I may share future posts on hiking, beaches, and bike trails. I'm overwhelmed with so many recommendations and committed to transitioning from newcomer to experienced New Englander while sharing travel experiences along the way.
