Mallorca is one of those islands that makes you feel like you're in a dream. I've had so many 'pinch me' moments here, from the breathtaking landscape and beaches to the beautiful architecture in Palma.

## What to do in Mallorca

Palma is the capital and features some of the most beautiful architecture in Spain. The narrow streets and distinctive buildings with balconies, shutters, and stonework reflect Mediterranean aesthetics beautifully. The Catedral-Basílica de Santa María de Mallorca serves as a notable Gothic landmark that visitors can explore via guided tours or self-guided visits.

The Banys Àrabs are the remains of the Arab baths from the time of Moorish rule.

### Take the train to Soller

A historical train departs from Palma and journeys through the mountains to the city of Soller, with optional tram connections to Port of Soller. This option suits travelers without vehicle access.

### Relax on breathtaking beaches

Mallorca is known for its beaches, though accessibility can be challenging without transportation. Cala Llombards is my favorite, about an hour from Palma by car.

### Visit the Drach Caves

Located in Porto Cristo, this destination features one of the biggest underground lakes in the world with a live orchestra that plays on a boat in the middle of the lake.

I recommend skipping the Hams Caves as they are much smaller and the experience is less impressive.

## Where to Eat in Mallorca

### Restaurant es Grau

Positioned on top of a cliff overlooking the sea, this establishment offers incredible views and quality cuisine.

### El Rinconcito del Norte

A small hole in the wall in the heart of Palma, this Spanish restaurant features great food and service. Advance reservations are essential due to very limited seating.

### Satrigo

An Italian restaurant in Portopí offering great pizza and garlic bread plus spicy chili oil.

### El Puente

A popular Mediterranean restaurant in the Santa Catalina area of Palma providing Spanish and Mediterranean cuisine.

## Where to stay in Mallorca

### Catalonia Majorica

This hotel overlooks Palma city and features an outdoor pool and deck with incredible views. It's about a 45 minute walk from the city center.

I advise avoiding Magaluf unless prioritizing nightlife—it's a little too touristy, not very family friendly, and distant from Palma.

I also warn against HSM Atlantic Park where I previously stayed, due to poor meal quality and nighttime noise issues.
