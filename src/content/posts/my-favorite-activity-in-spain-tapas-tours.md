Whenever I travel somewhere new, my goal is to experience as much of the culture as I can while I'm there. Food serves as my preferred gateway to understanding new destinations, and I avoid typical tourist establishments in favor of authentic local venues.

A local will show you popular local spots, which usually end up being little hole-in-the-walls that you would never be able to find on your own. The tours usually consist of small groups up to 12 or so to make the experience more intimate.

I've explored tapas experiences exclusively in Spain so far, though food tours apply broadly across travel destinations.

For finding such experiences, I really like GetYourGuide but both GetYourGuide and Viator work great. Both platforms allow users to search and purchase activities through their websites or mobile applications.

So next time you're traveling in Spain and want to experience local cuisine, check out a tapas tour and let me know what you think!
