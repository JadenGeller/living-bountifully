There is so much to do in Barcelona, and sometimes there's just too little time. That was exactly the case for me when I visited last year. My cousin and I arrived from Mallorca that morning and flew to Sevilla the following evening.

With limited time, here are my top three recommendations for maximizing a brief stay.

## Admire the Sagrada Familia

Barcelona's star attraction, the Sagrada Familia, attracts visitors from all around the world. Antoni Gaudi was one of the prominent architects of this masterpiece, but his influence in Barcelona doesn't stop there, so more on him later.

A word of advice, please do not wait to buy tickets until the last minute like I did. They ended up being sold out and we were only able to admire the basilica from the outside, which was still incredible, but we definitely missed out on a big part of the experience.

I recommend the Cathedral of Barcelona as an alternative, featuring a rooftop terrace with exceptional views of the city.

## Take a Guided Tapas Tour in the Gothic Quarter

If you've read any of my blog posts, you probably knew this one was coming. It's my absolute favorite thing to do in Spain, and who knows, maybe you'll make some friends along the way.

Tapas tours offer cultural insight and access to local establishments while eliminating the planning burden.

## Tour One of Gaudi's Many Architectural Masterpieces

Like I mentioned earlier, Gaudi had a huge impact in Barcelona. He was an architect and designer known to have an exponential impact on the modernism movement, specifically Catalan Modernism.

Options include:

- Park Guell
- Casa Vicens Gaudi
- La Pedrera–Casa Mila
- Casa Batlló
